# Legacy Hub Directors

## Legacy v1 Director model

Each employee business seat has exactly one Director. That Director is the employee's only Legacy front door and owns the complete workflow for that seat. There is no central super Director.

Directors may coordinate directly with one another when needed. This does not change the employee's assigned Director or transfer ownership of another seat's workflow.

## Included Directors

| Business seat | Director | Primary workflow ownership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | **Owner Director** | Owner priorities, approvals, business visibility, and cross-seat decisions requiring owner authority |
| Design & Sales | **Design & Sales Director** | Consultations, design/sales capture, proposals, follow-up, and design/sales customer communication |
| Account Manager | **Account Manager Director** | Customer relationship, property management, evaluations, customer communication, scheduling coordination, and opportunities |
| Crew Leader | **Crew Leader Director** | Daily crew management, field capture, job-progress updates, site issues, and field handoffs |

## Director operating contract

Every Director:

1. Receives and clarifies requests from the employee in its assigned business seat.
2. Owns the complete business workflow for that seat, including handoffs and exceptions.
3. Has direct access to Airtable, Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Calendar, subject to role permissions.
4. Is responsible for customer communication, scheduling, calendar management, task management, role-appropriate business decisions, and Airtable updates.
5. Reads and organizes relevant Google Drive records and files.
6. Sends and receives Gmail and records material workflow outcomes in Airtable.
7. Performs work directly whenever possible.
8. Requests a Specialist Agent only when its bounded expertise is necessary.
9. Interprets the specialist result, makes the role-appropriate next decision, and completes the next action.

## Director-to-Director coordination

| Situation | Coordination rule |
| --- | --- |
| Proposal needs property history | Design & Sales Director requests the relevant context from Account Manager Director. |
| Crew finds a customer-impacting site issue | Crew Leader Director records the issue and coordinates with Account Manager Director; customer communication remains with the accountable Director. |
| Decision exceeds seat authority | The Director creates an approval task for Owner Director with the facts, recommendation, and required decision. |
| Work crosses multiple seats | Each Director owns its portion; linked Airtable tasks and records make the handoff visible. |

## Specialist Agent boundary

Specialist Agents are shared services, not Directors and not employee-facing assistants. A Director sends a bounded request with the relevant record, source links, constraints, and desired output. The specialist returns a structured result to that requesting Director only.

The Director—not the Specialist Agent—owns the decision, Airtable update, customer communication, calendar/task change, and workflow completion.

## Future Releases

The following are not Legacy v1 Directors: a separate AI Director, Operations Director, Financial Intelligence Director, and any additional seat Director. They may be evaluated only as future business seats and releases.
