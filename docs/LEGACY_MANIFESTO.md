LEGACY MANIFESTO
Purpose

Legacy Hub is the AI operating system for All Landscape.

Its purpose is to provide every employee with an AI Director that understands their role, assists with daily work, and coordinates specialized AI capabilities while maintaining structured business data.

Principle 1
One Seat = One Director

Every business seat has exactly one Director.

Examples:

Owner Director
Design / Sales Director
Account Manager Director
Crew Leader Director

The employee communicates only with their Director.

The Director is the front door.

Principle 2
Directors Own the Business

Directors are responsible for:

Business decisions
Workflow management
Customer communication
Task management
Scheduling
Quotes
Invoices
Using connected applications
Delegating specialized work

A Director owns the complete workflow for its seat.

Principle 3
Specialist Agents Provide Expertise

Agents do not own workflows.

Agents do not communicate directly with employees.

Agents provide specialized expertise.

Examples:

Plant Library Agent
Design Agent
Photo Library Agent
Financial Analysis Agent

Agents always return structured results to the requesting Director.

Principle 4
Airtable is the Source of Truth

All structured business data lives in Airtable.

Examples:

Customers
Properties
Tasks
Workflow Status
Quotes
Evaluations
Plant Library
Dashboard Data
Principle 5
Google Drive Stores Files

Google Drive stores:

Photos
Designs
Documents
Contracts
Reports
Mood Boards

Airtable stores references to those files.

Principle 6
Connected Apps

Directors use ChatGPT-connected applications directly.

Including:

Airtable
Google Drive
Gmail
Google Calendar
Principle 7
External Systems

Only two systems are external:

Make
Jobber

Jobber synchronization occurs through Make.

No other business logic belongs in Make.

Principle 8
Structured Data

Every workflow produces structured data.

Directors exchange structured information.

Free-form conversation is for people.

Structured records are for Legacy.

Principle 9
Shared Services

Specialist Agents are shared services.

Any Director can use them.

No Specialist Agent belongs to a single Director.

Principle 10
Simplicity First

Legacy should remain understandable.
